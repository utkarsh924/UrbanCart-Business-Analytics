# UrbanCart — E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

UrbanCart is an e-commerce business analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, marketing channels, returns, and delivery operations.

The project combines Excel and MySQL to transform raw business data into actionable business insights.

## Business Objectives

- Measure overall sales and profitability
- Identify high-performing categories and products
- Analyse marketing channel performance
- Understand customer and payment behaviour
- Analyse returns and delivery performance
- Identify low-margin and improvement areas
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- MySQL
- PowerPoint
- Pivot Tables & Charts
- SQL Queries
- Business Intelligence & Data Analysis

## Key Results

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Net Sales | ₹68.61 Lakh |
| Net Profit | ₹16.34 Lakh |
| Profit Margin | 23.81% |
| Total Orders | 2,500 |
| Delivered Orders | 2,028 |
| Return Rate | 11.98% |

## Key Business Insights

- Electronics generated strong sales and profit performance.
- Organic marketing was the strongest acquisition channel by net sales.
- Returning customers generated higher net sales and profit than new customers.
- Some high-sales products had relatively low profit margins.
- Size/Fit Issues and Damaged Products were major return reasons.
- Delivery delays represent an important operational improvement area.

## Business Value

The analysis helps management understand where revenue and profit are being generated, where margin and operational issues exist, and which areas can be prioritised for business improvement.

## Conclusion

UrbanCart demonstrates an end-to-end business analytics workflow — from structured business data and SQL analysis to Excel dashboards and management-level recommendations.
